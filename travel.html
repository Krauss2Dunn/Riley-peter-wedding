Travel & Lodging

Getting to the Gorge.

Wind Mountain Ranch sits above the Columbia River Gorge in Stevenson, Washington — about an hour from Portland. The area is known for waterfalls, vineyards, and sweeping views of the river valley.

We recommend flying into Portland International Airport and renting a car. It is a stunning one-hour drive from Portland to the Hood River area.

Taxis and Ubers are not common in the Hood River area, so having a car will give you flexibility during the weekend.

We will have shuttles to and from the wedding location and hotels.

Airport

The closest major airport is Portland International Airport, approximately one hour from Stevenson.

Stevenson

Stevenson sits on the Washington side of the Gorge and offers beautiful river views and quiet mountain surroundings.

Hood River

Just across the river, Hood River offers restaurants, breweries, and additional lodging options.

Where to Stay

Our Strong Recommendation

Stay at Skamania Lodge

We strongly recommend booking early at Skamania Lodge in Stevenson, Washington. Ten minutes down the road from the venue, it is a beautiful resort with golf, disc golf, ziplining, treehouses, and enough other activities to make a long weekend out of it—or at least justify arriving before the bar opens.

Skamania will be the main shuttle pick-up and drop-off location for the wedding, so staying there is by far the easiest option. More importantly, the more of our wedding crowd that books there, the closer we get to a full Krauss-Dunn takeover.

We do not currently have a room block or special rate, regrettably, but prices are still fairly reasonable. We are also working with the lodge to secure a few perks or discounts for our guests. When booking, please reference the Krauss-Dunn Group so they know you are with us—and so we can continue making a compelling case for said perks.

Pro tip: Families and groups should take a look at the Tree House Suites. They are objectively pretty sweet.

Best Western Plus Columbia River Inn

Because Peter was shamefully unable to lock down a discounted rate at Skamania, we understand that it’s a pretty penny to drop on our weekend. As such, we recommend the Best Western Plus Columbia River Inn for a more economical alternative.

The Best Western sits smack on the river, just beside the Bridge of the Gods. This is by no means slumming it. It’s a beautiful spot, very close to our after-party location and the lovely Thunder Island park at Cascade Locks.

Tip: For both hotels, we recommend calling and asking what discounts they are running. Both locations currently have deals that can get up to 30% off, so be brave and pick up the phone.
